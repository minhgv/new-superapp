# Research Methodology

## Scope

Define a reusable standard for a mini app store in a super app: publisher onboarding, catalog/discovery, package/runtime compatibility, permissions, review, supply-chain security, release, rollout, rollback, operations, analytics, monetization, localization and deprecation.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Normative standards and primary government guidance.
2. Official platform/vendor documentation.
3. Primary project specifications and official engineering references.
4. Independent benchmark/context sources.
5. Explicit proposals derived from the evidence.

Search results were treated as leads only. URLs in the evidence set were rechecked by the parent orchestrator; the validation summary records the result.

## Safety and privacy

The Google Sheet was read for platform context only. Credentials, account details and private access instructions were excluded from the report, evidence and repository.
